01 — The Mandate

Every person has a birthright to hear the Gospel at least once. Karl Hargestam serves the global movement of the Pentecostal Church of God as its Global Missions Director — a servant of the movement, never its owner. His assignment: to strengthen national churches and pioneer teams in the unfinished work among the unreached, until every tribe, tongue, and nation has had its chance.

02 — The Charter

I.One Mission

That every person hears the Gospel at least once — the birthright of every human being.

II.One Movement

A worldwide fellowship of national churches — decentralized, indigenous, and owned by no one man.

III.One Chance

One life. One hearing. One opportunity to respond — the public call that mobilizes the Church toward the final frontier.

03 — The Movement He Serves

A global fellowship, not a personal platform.

Karl serves the Pentecostal Church of God — a worldwide fellowship whose formal conference family spans 78 nations, touching almost half the world’s nations through a century of planting, training, and shared mission. The research into that footprint continues; only what can be verified is reported.

Growth here is decentralized by conviction: national movements leading their own nations, multiplying churches, training their own workers. OneChance schools are now spreading through PCG churches across the nations — fruit of the movement, belonging to the movement.

05 — History

The road here.

Stated plainly, as a matter of record. These were never sequential stops. PCG has carried through Helimission, Joshua Campaign, the farm, MOE, and CityServe alike, with One Chance for Every Person as the guiding light throughout.

Sweden

Born in Sweden, of Sami heritage on his grandmother’s side — a heritage he still votes to uphold in the Sametinget.

1989–1991

At 21, left Sweden for pioneer evangelism in Africa. Missionary to Tanzania, building logistics support across Tanzania, Burundi, Rwanda, Uganda and Congo.

1991–1997

Earned his helicopter wings: commercial training, flight instructor in America, then regional director of Helimission Ethiopia, flying the Bell in pioneer service.

1992

Married Jennifer.

1993

Ordained as a minister of the Pentecostal Church of God; mission appointment. PCG has carried every assignment since.

1998

Founded Joshua Campaign International; the first campaigns in Ethiopia. 4.2 million recorded decisions for Christ, and from 2007 an AStar helicopter serving Joshua Campaign Ethiopia, operated by Helimission.

1999–2009

Area supervisor, Pentecostal Church of God. Planted and established churches and denominations in Ethiopia and Kenya.

OCFEP

Founded One Chance for Every Person, serving as its president, the guiding light through every assignment.

2008–2019

Founder and co-owner, Joshua Flower Farms, Ethiopia. Commercial flower exports funding local church planters in the Horn of Africa.

2010

Founded Mission One Eleven; Founder, President and CEO. Expeditions, training, and aviation, flying the BO 105.

2016–2017

Head of Mission, Swedish Pingst. Strategic oversight realigning national mission toward unreached peoples.

2018–2025

Co-founded CityServe, the Church united to serve its city; compassion at a scale only the whole Body can carry.

2020

Operation 326 began: identifying the final frontier of unreached peoples.

Today

Serves as Global Missions Director of the Pentecostal Church of God.
